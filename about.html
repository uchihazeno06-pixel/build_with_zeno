Why I started Build with Zeno

I'm Zeno — 17, Nigerian, self-taught.

I didn't learn to code in a classroom. I learned by opening things up, breaking them, and figuring out how to put them back together — mostly because I couldn't afford the tools everyone else was paying for every month.

At some point I stopped seeing that as a limitation and started seeing it as the actual skill: if you understand how a tool works, you don't need to rent it. You can build it. That's the whole idea behind Build with Zeno — take the software people pay big tech for every month, and teach people how to build their own version, right in the browser.

This isn't a course you finish once. It's a subscription because the tools keep coming — new guides, new downloads, and a private WhatsApp community where members ask for exactly what they need built next.

If you're tired of watching your card get charged for things you could learn to build yourself, you're the person I built this for.
